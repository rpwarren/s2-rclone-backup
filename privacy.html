Privacy Policy: S2 rclone backup

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Who uses this app

Only its owner. The app is a personal backup tool and is not available to anyone else.

What Google data it accesses

The app uses the Google Drive API to upload, list and delete backup files in the owner's own Google Drive. It does not read any other Google data.

How the data is used

Files are stored in the owner's Drive solely so the owner can restore his own server. Nothing is collected, analyzed, sold, or shared with any third party, and no data leaves the owner's own server and Google account.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account security settings under third-party connections.
